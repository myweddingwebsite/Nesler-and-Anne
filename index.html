GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & Answers

When is the RSVP deadline?
Kindly confirm your attendance on or before November 20, 2026 through our RSVP page, so we can prepare your seat and meal.
Can I bring a plus one?
We have reserved a specific number of seats for you, as indicated when you search your name in the RSVP form. Due to limited venue capacity, we kindly ask that only the guests named on your invitation attend.
Are children allowed?
We love your little ones! However, apart from the children in our entourage, we can only accommodate those specifically included in your invitation.
What time should I arrive?
Guest seating starts at 3:00 PM. We recommend arriving at least 30 minutes early to find parking and settle in before the entourage procession begins.
What should I wear?
Formal or semi-formal attire in shades of wine, mauve, gold, nude or cocoa. Please avoid white, and opt for block heels as the venue is a garden. See the Dress Code section for details.
Is the ceremony and reception in one place?
Yes — both the ceremony and reception will be held at Viridis Countryside Garden, Amadeo, Cavite, so there’s no need to travel between venues.
Is there parking at the venue?
Parking is available at the venue. Carpooling is encouraged, and we suggest using Waze or Google Maps for the most accurate route to Amadeo.
Can I take photos during the ceremony?
We’d love for you to be fully present with us during the ceremony — our photographers will capture every moment. Feel free to take as many photos as you like during the cocktails and reception, and upload them to our shared album!
What gift should I give?
Your presence is truly enough. Should you wish to give, a monetary gift toward our future and business would be deeply appreciated. You may use the QR codes in our Gift Guide.
Who can I contact for questions?
Feel free to send a message directly to Nesler or Anne — we’ll be happy to help!
